Grocery delivery for Poland’s largest retailer

appBEAT client engagement · Aug 2024 – Mar 2026 · Mobile engineering lead

BIEK is the grocery-delivery app of Biedronka, Poland’s largest retailer, on iOS and Android. SYZYGY Warsaw invited me, through my consultancy appBEAT, to help win the tender for the mobile app.

I helped win the tender, built and led the core mobile team, co-created the architecture with fellow engineers and defined the API contracts. I also contributed code alongside my Doktor.se role.

What I did

Tender won, MVP scope agreed.
By assessing the RFP, estimating the work, holding the technical discussions with the business partners and presenting delivery options from an MVP to full scope, with the staffing, dependencies and trade-offs for each.
Logic written once, native UIs.
Business logic shared by iOS and Android with fully native SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose interfaces, by choosing Kotlin Multiplatform over Compose Multiplatform, then still in beta.
Months unblocked before the backend.
Complete shopping flows built and tested on in-memory providers, by defining the API contracts and a contract-first workflow. Moving to the real API meant swapping a provider, not rewriting screens.
Building the team.
I ran the hiring for the core mobile team, starting with a trusted domain expert, and onboarded the team into the new architecture so it could plan its own delivery timeline.
Hands-on code.
I co-created the shared Kotlin Multiplatform architecture, built the cart and the UI components library, authored several screens and modules, and integrated Zowie’s customer-service chatbot SDK.

Launch

By March 2026 the architecture, delivery processes, most features and backend contracts were in place. BIEK launched nationally on 9 November 2026.
