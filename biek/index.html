Grocery delivery for Poland’s largest retailer

appBEAT client engagement · Aug 2024 – Mar 2026 · Mobile engineering lead

BIEK is the grocery-delivery app of Biedronka, Poland’s largest retailer, on iOS and Android. SYZYGY Warsaw invited me, through my consultancy appBEAT, to help win the tender for the mobile app.

I helped win the tender, built and led the core mobile team and co-created the architecture with fellow engineers. I also contributed code alongside my Doktor.se role.

What I did

Winning the tender.
Assessed the RFP, estimated the work and held the technical discussions with the business partners; presented delivery options from an MVP to full scope, with the staffing, dependencies and trade-offs for each.
Shared logic, native interfaces.
We chose Kotlin Multiplatform to share business logic while keeping native interfaces on iOS and Android.
Building before the backend.
I defined API contracts and a workflow using in-memory data providers so we could build and test shopping flows for months before backend implementation began.
Building the team.
I ran the hiring for the core mobile team, starting with a trusted domain expert, and onboarded the team into the new architecture so it could plan its own delivery timeline.
Hands-on code.
I co-created the shared Kotlin Multiplatform architecture, built the cart and the UI components library, authored several screens and modules, and integrated Zowie’s customer-service chatbot SDK.

Launch

By March 2026 the architecture, delivery processes, most features and backend contracts were in place. BIEK launched nationally on 9 November 2026.
